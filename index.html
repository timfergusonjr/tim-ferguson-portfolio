Legal Services · Anonymized enterprise case study

Turning a $1M annual account into a $1.5M partnership.

Two years into a three-year agreement, more than $1M in purchased software remained unimplemented. Renewal risk was clear. The response began with a plan for customer value realization—not a last-minute negotiation.

+$500KIncremental annual contract value
+$1.5MIncremental three-year contract value (TCV)

The challenge

Recognize the risk early

I identified that the customer had purchased more than $1M in software but had implemented virtually none of it, despite being two years into a three-year contract. With renewal approaching, adoption gaps created significant commercial and relationship risk.

  • Surface the risk well ahead of renewal.
  • Understand the customer's intended strategic outcomes.
Financial Services · Anonymized enterprise case study

Aligning commercial investment with customer value.

An early renewal became an opportunity to support a broader IT Operations Management and IT Asset Management transformation—while ensuring the customer invested in software as it was ready to implement and realize value from it.

+$680KIncrease in annual contract value
60.7%Annual contract value growth
~$2.04MImplied incremental value over three years*
01
Build the champion

Cultivated an experienced customer advocate who helped shape the transformation, implementation roadmap, and purchasing plan.

02
Sequence for adoption

Aligned the purchase plan to the implementation sequence: ITOM first, followed by ITAM licenses as the customer progressed toward readiness and value realization.

03
Lead the commercial strategy

Led commercial structuring and procurement negotiations, creating a phased purchase model that reduced the time between paying for technology and being able to use it.

04
Deliver a mutual win

Secured the renewal nearly a year early and grew annual contract value from $1.12M to $1.8M, while aligning investment to the customer's business priorities and implementation milestones.

*Three-year value is an implied comparison based on ACV multiplied by three, not a verified signed TCV. The phased license schedule may make actual contracted value different.

Leadership attribution: I identified the risk, established the overall strategy, coordinated the appropriate ServiceNow stakeholders and partner, and personally drove the commercial strategy, negotiation, customer alignment, and execution cadence. Delivery was a collective effort involving many ServiceNow colleagues, the implementation partner, and customer stakeholders.
